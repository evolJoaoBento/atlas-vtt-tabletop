How should tokens and notes look on the 3D board?

Each picture shows the board tilted, the way you'd look at it in play. A token with a mini always shows the mini standing on its token disc. The question is tokens without a mini, and notes.

Tokens without a mini

A. Floating disc

The token's art as a thick coin, lying flat a little above the board, with a shadow under it. Closest to Atlas.

B. Standee

The token's art as an upright cardboard cut-out on a round base, like a paper mini. Reads as a figure from low angles.

C. Floating badge

The round token art always turned to face you, hovering over its spot with a shadow dot below. Easiest to read from any angle.

Notes

A. Map pin with label

A floating pin over the note's spot, its title above. Click opens the note in a new tab.

B. Floating paper

A small sheet of parchment hovering over the spot, slightly turned to you, title on top.

C. Atlas's own icon

The same note icon Atlas draws, floating and facing you, title on hover. Looks like Atlas, just lifted.
